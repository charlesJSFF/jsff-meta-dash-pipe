IMPORTANT: The asterisk (*) suffix on some metric names in the docs
indicates the metric is refreshed several times during the day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_total_actions
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of clicks on your Page's contact info and call-to-action button.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: total_interactions (Account Insights)
  Confidence: likely equivalent
  Notes: total_interactions in IG Account Insights combines likes, comments, shares, saves, profile visits, and follows. Documentation describes it as 'Total number of interactions with your Instagram account.'

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_post_engagements
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times people have engaged with your posts through reactions, comments, shares and more.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day

  Instagram Equivalent: engagement (Media Insights) or total_interactions (Account Insights)
  Confidence: close equivalent
  Notes: engagement on IG Media = likes + comments + saves + video_views/plays. total_interactions is the account-level aggregate. IG does not have a single 'post engagements' metric that combines everything across all posts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_daily_follows
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    Not defined
  Supported Period(s): day
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day; Description says 'Not defined' in Meta docs (follows metrics)

  Instagram Equivalent: follows (Account Insights)
  Confidence: direct equivalent
  Notes: IG follows metric tracks new follows (not net). Period: day, week, days_28. No 'unique' variant available.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_daily_follows_unique
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    Not defined This metric is estimated .
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day; Unique-user metric (deduplicated); Description says 'Not defined' in Meta docs (follows metrics); Estimated metric (based on modeling, not exact count)

  Instagram Equivalent: follows (Account Insights) — IG doesn't separate unique vs total for follows
  Confidence: partial equivalent
  Notes: Page Insights splits follows by unique; IG only has total follows. The IG metric most closely approximates page_daily_follows (total).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_daily_unfollows_unique
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of Meta Accounts that unfollowed your Page in the selected time period. This metric is estimated .
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day; Unique-user metric (deduplicated); Estimated metric (based on modeling, not exact count)

  Instagram Equivalent: no equivalent found
  Confidence: no known IG metric
  Notes: IG Insights does not expose unfollows or unfollow counts as a metric, unique or otherwise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_follows
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of followers of your Facebook Page or profile. This is calculated as the number of follows minus the number of unfollows over the lifetime of your Facebook Page or profile.
  Supported Period(s): day
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day

  Instagram Equivalent: follower_count (Account Insights)
  Confidence: direct equivalent
  Notes: follower_count = total current followers (cumulative). This is a snapshot metric, not a delta. Period: day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_media_view
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times your content was played or displayed. Content includes videos, posts, stories and ads.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Source: Page View Metrics table (nonstandard structure). Breakdowns: is_from_ads, is_from_followers

  Instagram Equivalent: plays (Media Insights — Reels) or video_views (Media Insights — Videos)
  Confidence: partial equivalent
  Notes: IG has separate metrics for photo views (impressions) vs video plays/views. No single 'media view' aggregate that covers all content types (images + video + stories).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_total_media_view_unique
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of total unique media viewers for FB page.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Source: Page View Metrics table (nonstandard structure). Breakdowns: (not specified)

  Instagram Equivalent: reach (Media Insights or Account Insights)
  Confidence: partial equivalent
  Notes: reach on IG = unique accounts that saw the content. Close to 'unique viewers' concept. For account-level: reach. For per-media: reach (unique accounts that saw that post).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_actions_post_reactions_total
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    Daily total post reactions of a page by type.
  Supported Period(s): day
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: likes + comments + saved + shares (Media Insights — individual metrics)
  Confidence: partial equivalent
  Notes: IG separates likes, comments, saves, shares into individual metrics rather than a single 'reactions' total. There is no combined 'reactions' or 'total actions' per-post metric.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_video_views
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times your Page's videos played for at least 3 seconds, or for nearly their total length if they're shorter than 3 seconds. During a single instance of a video playing, we'll exclude any time spent replaying the video.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: plays (Media Insights — Reels) or video_views (Media Insights — Videos)
  Confidence: direct equivalent
  Notes: IG video_views (Video Posts): count of 3-second+ views. IG plays (Reels): count of initial plays. Different thresholds from FB Page video views (3-second depending on context).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_video_views_paid
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times your Page's promoted videos played for at least 3 seconds, or for nearly their total length if they're shorter than 3 seconds. For each impression of a video, we'll count video views separately and exclude any time spent replaying the video.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: no equivalent found
  Confidence: no known IG metric
  Notes: IG Insights does not split video views by paid vs organic in the public available metrics. The paid/organic breakdown is available in Ads Manager, not the Insights API.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_video_views_organic
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times your Page's videos played for at least 3 seconds, or for nearly their total length if they're shorter than 3 seconds, by organic reach. During a single instance of a video playing, we'll exclude any time spent replaying the video.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: no equivalent found
  Confidence: no known IG metric
  Notes: IG Insights does not split video views by paid vs organic in the public available metrics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_video_view_time
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The total time, in milliseconds, people viewed your Page's video.
  Supported Period(s): day
  Caveats: None documented in table

  Instagram Equivalent: video_views_time_played (Media Insights — Video)
  Confidence: likely equivalent
  Notes: IG Video Posts have video_views_time_played_total_ms as a per-media metric. However, there is no aggregate account-level video view time metric. This would need to be summed across individual media.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRIC: page_views_total
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official Description:
    The number of times a Page's profile has been viewed by logged in and logged out people.
  Supported Period(s): day, week, days_28
  Caveats: Asterisk (*) = metric refreshed several times during the day

  Instagram Equivalent: profile_views (Account Insights)
  Confidence: partial equivalent
  Notes: profile_views = total profile visits in IG Account Insights. page_views_total is broader — it includes all Page tab views (timeline, photos, about, etc.), not just the main profile view.
